### Report a problem

Use the subject **Captain Strawberry Support** and include:

- Your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.
- The app version.
- What happened and what you expected to happen.
- The steps needed to reproduce the problem.
- A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue. Please hide any personal information.

Never send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

### Quick troubleshooting

If the app does not open, freezes, or closes unexpectedly:

1. Close the app and open it again.
2. Check the App Store for an available app update.
3. Restart your device.
4. If the problem continues, email us with the details listed above.
